The Residence

A masterclass in private, versatile living — designed around the view.

Set on nearly an acre of sloping ridge, this newly built estate offers what almost no home in Nebraska can: rare, panoramic views of a private 150-acre lake. The architecture is deliberate and quiet. Glass meets stone. Cantilevered decks meet open prairie. Every primary room frames water.

The entire upper level is reserved as the Owners' Wing — a private retreat with sweeping lake views, a spa-grade bathroom, and direct access to a cantilevered terrace built for sunsets. The main level is given over to gathering: a chef's kitchen with paneled appliances, a great room anchored by a stone hearth, and a covered loggia that opens to an expanded lawn.

The Setting

Working sunrise. Lake-fed sunset.

Lonergan Ridge sits north of Omaha along a wildlife corridor of rolling Nebraska hills. The lake itself — 150 private acres — is shared by a small community of estate owners and is closed to public access. Kayak, paddleboard, and fish from your own dock.

The home faces due west. From the great room, sunrise reflects off the bluffs behind the residence. From the upper terrace, sunset settles over open water.
